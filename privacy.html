Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

"Je peux ?" is a personal Android application developed for the private use of a single person, its developer. It is not distributed to the public.

Data processed

The application reads the balance and the transactions of the developer's own bank accounts, through the Enable Banking API, in order to compute how much money can be spent until the next paycheck.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the developer's phone, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. The application has no server. No data is sent to any third party, other than the requests made directly from the phone to Enable Banking and to the bank in order to read the accounts.

Sharing and retention

Data is not shared, sold or used for advertising. It can be deleted at any time from the application settings, which also closes the bank session and erases the stored key.

Contact

The developer can be contacted through the repository hosting this page.
